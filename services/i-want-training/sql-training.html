Stop Waiting on IT Tickets

Empower your business teams to answer their own questions. We teach them to query your data directly and safely.

The Problem: "The Data Bottleneck"

The Queue: Marketing needs a list. Sales needs a number. They file a ticket. IT takes 2 weeks to respond. Opportunity lost.

The Solution: "Data Democracy"

Self-Sufficiency: Your team writes a simple `SELECT` statement and gets the data in 3 minutes. No tickets. No waiting.

Learn on YOUR data

We don't teach abstract theory using a "Movie Store" database. We connect to your sandbox and teach your team how to query your actual tables.

  • Window Functions: Calculating running totals and moving averages.

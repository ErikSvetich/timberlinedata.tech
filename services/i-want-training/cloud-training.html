Master the Modern Stack

Snowflake and Databricks are powerful, but they are expensive if used incorrectly. We teach your team to build without breaking the bank.

The Problem: "The Money Pit"

The Bill Shock: Your team treats the cloud like an on-premise server. They run inefficient queries that leave warehouses spinning 24/7.

The Solution: "Cost-Aware Code"

The Optimization: We teach "Partitioning," "Clustering," and "Warehousing Strategy" so you only pay for the compute you actually use.

Platform Agnostic

Whether you are on the "Data Lakehouse" (Databricks) or the "Data Cloud" (Snowflake), the principles of performance tuning are universal.

  • Architecture: Deep dive into Compute vs. Storage separation.
  • Performance Tuning: How to spot and fix slow-running queries.
  • Governance: Managing access controls (RBAC) and data masking.
  • Data Engineering: Building robust pipelines using dbt, Snowpipe, or Delta Live Tables.

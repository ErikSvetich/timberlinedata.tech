Erik Svetich

Founder & Principal Consultant

I founded Timberline Data after 13 years of building analytics at some of the largest companies in the Pacific Northwest. The pattern was always the same: smaller businesses needed the same caliber of data work but couldn't justify a full-time hire. That's the gap we fill.

We believe data shouldn't be a black box. Whether you are a small business looking to automate inventory, or a mid-size company scaling your cloud infrastructure, we build solutions that are transparent, effective, and profitable.

When I'm not writing SQL, you can find me hiking Mt. Hood or exploring the PNW with my family.

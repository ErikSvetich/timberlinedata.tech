About Timberline Data

Erik Svetich - Principal Consultant

With years of experience at industry giants like Nike, I founded Timberline Data to bring enterprise-grade analytics to the greater Portland area.

I believe that data shouldn't be a black box. Whether you are a small business looking to automate inventory, or a medium enterprise scaling your cloud infrastructure, I build solutions that are transparent, effective, and profitable.

When I'm not writing SQL, you can find me hiking Mt. Hood or exploring the PNW.
